What the evidence supports

  • Adding emb changes fused top-1 by +0.9 pts (95% CI -1.6 to +3.1; McNemar p = 0.58); the interval includes zero, so the gain is not statistically established at this sample size.
  • It is right on 35 sentences the classifier misses, raising the oracle top-1 of clf from 0.760 to 0.838.
  • Probability quality: NLL 0.609 -> 0.583.
  • The curated phrases outperform 100% of random same-size phrase sets (supporting that they are better than arbitrary exemplars).
